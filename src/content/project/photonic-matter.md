Photonic function makes self-assembly a particularly sharp design problem: a structure must be accessible from realistic building blocks, robust to defects, and optically useful once it forms.

My work explores both ordered and disordered routes. That includes diamond-related and gyroid crystals with complete photonic band gaps, as well as photonic glasses where short-range correlations can be tuned to control colour without strong angle dependence.
