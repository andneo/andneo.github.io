Biological assembly is an opportunity to test whether the statistical-mechanical ideas developed for synthetic materials survive in systems that are heterogeneous, noisy and strongly coupled to function.

My current work in this direction focuses on viral capsid assembly around RNA, where changing protein concentration can redirect the system from well-formed particles toward malformed intermediates and extended condensates.
