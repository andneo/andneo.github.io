Disordered matter is not one thing. A liquid can remain metastable far below its melting point, become dynamically arrested into a glass, or even stabilise an amorphous structure thermodynamically. I use statistical mechanics and simulation to understand which microscopic features distinguish these possibilities.

A major thread is the potential-energy landscape: reducing a complex many-body system to the statistics of its basins provides a route from microscopic configurations to equations of state, phase behaviour and low-temperature thermodynamics. I am also interested in how this viewpoint connects to deliberately designed amorphous materials.
