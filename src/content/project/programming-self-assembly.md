The central problem is not simply whether particles attract, but whether their interactions encode enough information to select the right collective organisation while avoiding competing structures and kinetic traps.

I am particularly interested in design rules that operate beyond a single crystal unit cell: controlling ring statistics, assembly pathways and frustration so that the target can be crystalline, amorphous or somewhere between the two.
