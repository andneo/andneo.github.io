abstract: >-
  A design strategy for amorphous self-assembly is introduced by encoding frustration directly into the interactions between building blocks. The programmed particles favour five-membered rings and assemble into a thermodynamically stable disordered network of face-sharing dodecahedral cages rather than a kinetically arrested glass.
