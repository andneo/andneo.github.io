abstract: >-
  The potential-energy landscape of the machine-learned coarse-grained ML-BOP model of water is shown to be accurately described by Gaussian landscape statistics over a broad density range. A landscape-based free-energy description then predicts the model's liquid–liquid transition and links microscopic inherent structures to thermodynamics.
