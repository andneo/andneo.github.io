abstract: >-
  A reverse-engineering and simulation strategy is used to design patchy colloidal rods that self-assemble through tetrahedral clusters into diamond-related photonic crystals. The resulting cubic and hexagonal polymorphs have overlapping photonic band gaps, making optical function unusually robust to stacking faults.
