abstract: >-
  A two-state Gibbs free-energy model is fitted to density and internal-energy data for the coarse-grained ML-BOP water model. The description predicts a liquid–liquid critical point and clarifies how low-density liquid fluctuations, spinodal decomposition and crystallisation are coupled in the supercooled regime.
