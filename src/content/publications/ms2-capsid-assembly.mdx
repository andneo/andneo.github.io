abstract: >-
  Experiments and coarse-grained Monte Carlo simulations show how increasing coat-protein concentration redirects MS2 assembly from well-formed virus-like particles to malformed multi-capsid structures and finally RNA–protein condensates. The results connect nucleation kinetics and protein-mediated bridging to biological assembly outcomes.
