abstract: >-
  A network liquid that densifies through successive liquid–liquid phase transitions is analysed through the geometry and topology of its rings. Densification is driven by the formation of linked rings, revealing a hierarchy of topological transitions and suggesting entanglement as a general mechanism for packing physical networks.
