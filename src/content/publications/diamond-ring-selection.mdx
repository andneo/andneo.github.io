abstract: >-
  A two-component tetrahedral patchy-particle system is designed so that bonding selects even-membered rings. This simple rule widens the range of interaction parameters over which diamond crystallisation is both thermodynamically and kinetically accessible, providing a scalable route to open colloidal crystals.
