abstract: >-
  Photonic glasses can produce structural colour with weak angle dependence, but often at the cost of saturation. This work uses multiple-scattering Monte Carlo calculations and controlled structure factors to show how the first peak of the structure factor and sample thickness govern that trade-off, providing design guidance for angle-independent structural colour.
