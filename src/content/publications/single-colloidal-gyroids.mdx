abstract: >-
  Two rationally designed families of chiral patchy spheres are shown to self-assemble into single-network gyroid crystals. The resulting structures and their inverses support complete photonic band gaps together with rich chiroptical responses, establishing single gyroids as promising self-assembled photonic materials.
